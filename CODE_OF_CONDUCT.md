# Community conduct

ANIE welcomes reproducible security research, constructive disagreement and respectful technical contributions. Do not harass, threaten or discriminate against participants. Keep research claims evidence-backed and acknowledge limitations. Maintainers may moderate inappropriate content.

Never post real-world targets, exploits, secrets, personal information, AEGIS/SNOOP private artifacts or vulnerability details in public issues. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private reporting guidance.
